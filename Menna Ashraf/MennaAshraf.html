Pain au chocolat

Few things evoke the charm of a French café quite like Pain au Chocolat. These delicate, buttery pastries are enveloped in layers of flaky dough with a decadent chocolate core, golden layers giving way to a soft, making every bite a delightful indulgence. [⤷Prep: 30 min | Cook: 20 min | Servings: 8]

- Ingredients:
    
for 8 servings
4 cups flour(500 g)
½ cup water(120 mL)
½ cup milk(120 mL)
¼ cup sugar(50 g)
2 teaspoons salt
1 packet instant dry yeast
3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
1 ¼ cups cold unsalted butter(285 g), cut into ½-inch (1 cm) thick slices
1 egg, beaten
2 bars sweetened chocolate bar


Ratatouille

Ratatouille is a simple peasant vegetable stew of French origin, that uses fresh in-season summer produce, like eggplants, tomatoes, zucchinis, and peppers. Herbs and spices play a great role in this dish as they help enhance the flavors of the humble vegetables. [⤷Prep: 15 min | Cook: 30 min | Servings: 8]

- Ingredients:

for 8 servings
2 eggplants
6 roma tomatoes
2 yellow squashes
2 zucchinis
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 medium white onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 red, yellow bell pepper, seeded and diced                                  
28 oz crushed tomatoes(795 g)
2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh basil


Creme brulee

Indulge in this rich and creamy dessert, made with silky smooth custard and a crispy caramelized sugar top. This classic French dessert is sure to impress with its decadent taste and elegant presentation.[⤷Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Servings: 6]

- Ingredients:

for 6 servings
4 cups heavy cream(960 mL)
2 teaspoons vanilla extract                                            
6 egg yolks
1 cup sugar(200 g), half in mixture, half for crust

Special Equipment
6 oz ramekins, 7 ounces (225 grams)
