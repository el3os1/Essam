Italy

Italy is a beautiful and historically rich country located in Southern Europe,
known for its stunning landscapes,
cultural heritage, and world-famous cuisine.

Cuisine

Italian cuisine is one of the most beloved and influential culinary traditions in the world.
It's known for its emphasis on fresh, high-quality ingredients,
simple preparation methods,
and regional diversity.

Main Dish

Spaghetti Carbonara

Spaghetti Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish that originated in the Lazio region, particularly in Rome.

lasagna bolognese

Lasagna Bolognese is a classic Italian dish, especially popular in the region of Emilia-Romagna (particularly Bologna). It's known for its rich, savory flavor and layered structure.

Pizza

Pizza is a dish of Italian origin consisting of a flat, round base of dough typically topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of other ingredients, then baked in an oven. It’s eaten as a main course and is loved all over the world.

Appetizers

Bruschetta

Bruschetta is a traditional Italian starter dish made of grilled or toasted bread topped with a variety of ingredients most commonly a mixture of tomatoes, garlic, basil, and olive oil. It originates from central Italy, particularly Tuscany and Lazio, and was originally a way for farmers to sample their freshly pressed olive oil.

Arancini

Arancini are deep-fried rice balls, typically stuffed with savory fillings like ragù, cheese, and peas, then breaded and fried to a golden crisp. They originate from Sicily, and the name "arancini" literally means "little oranges" in Italian, because of their round shape and golden color once fried.

Caprese Salad

Caprese Salad is a fresh, cold salad made of sliced tomatoes, fresh mozzarella cheese, basil leaves, and extra virgin olive oil. It originates from the island of Capri, off the coast of southern Italy, hence the name.

Desserts

Tiramisù

Tiramisù is a classic Italian layered dessert made of: Coffee-soaked ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi) Creamy mascarpone mixture Cocoa powder dusted on top It’s known for being rich, creamy, slightly bitter from coffee and cocoa, and subtly sweet.

Panna Cotta

Panna Cotta means "cooked cream" in Italian. It’s a smooth, creamy dessert made by gently heating sweetened cream, often with vanilla, and then setting it with gelatin. It’s served chilled, usually with a fruit sauce, caramel, or chocolate topping.

Sfogliatella

Sfogliatella (pronounced sfoh-lyah-TELL-ah) is a traditional Italian pastry from Naples, known for its crisp, shell-like layers and rich, sweet filling. The name comes from “sfoglia,” meaning "thin leaf/layer", referring to its many delicate layers.
