Tortilla de patatas con cebolla

Made with potatoes, eggs, onions, and a generous sprinkle of love, Tortilla de Patatas con Cebolla is Spain's ultimate student favorite. Affordable, quick to prepare, and deliciously filling, this classic Spanish omelet is perfect for breakfast

Paella

Born in Valencia, authentic paella combines bomba rice, saffron, fresh seafood, and seasonal vegetables. This iconic dish transforms simple ingredients into an absolute masterpiece

Churros

Crispy on the outside, pillowy-soft inside, dusted with cinnamon sugar and paired with thick, velvety chocolate, churros turn breakfast into celebration.

The Spanish word for 'sugar,' azúcar, is very similar to the Arabic word (sukkar).
